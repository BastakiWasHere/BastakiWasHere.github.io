## Why I chose Iceland


I chose Iceland because it has many distinctive features for a small country. I found the contrast between its name, “Iceland,” and its volcanoes especially interesting. Glaciers, hot springs, and waterfalls also caught my attention. Before beginning the project, I had noticed the importance of the coast to where people live. These interests led me to a question: how does Iceland's landscape relate to where people live and travel?
